Phone Alarm

A small hardware project built to reduce phone use with a physical stand, ultrasonic sensing, and simple control logic.

Problem & Approach

Design Goal

The goal was simple: spend less time on my phone by making a physical system that made phone pickup more intentional. Instead of relying on an app blocker, I wanted a small stand that could detect when the phone was removed and trigger an alarm response.

The system used an ultrasonic detector to monitor whether the phone was present in the stand. When the phone was removed, the Arduino logic changed state and activated the alarm behavior.

Engineering Work

Stability & Controls

I did calculations to make sure the phone would not tip or fall out of the stand during normal use. The geometry had to keep the phone's center of mass inside the support footprint while still leaving room for the ultrasonic sensor to read consistently.

The project also gave me hands-on practice with soldering, wiring reliability, sensor calibration, and simple control logic. The final build connected mechanical design choices directly to electronics behavior.
